`maximum_open_order_age`, `maximum_quote_age`, `maximum_state_skew`, and

## Context Projection

`RiskEvaluationContext` retains the complete released objects internally. Its
bounded projection contains:

- schema and context fingerprint;
- Order Intent and source-signal fingerprints;
- resolution time, mapping fingerprint, canonical instrument ID, and canonical
  descriptor fingerprint;
- separate cash, position, and open-order account fingerprints;
- four snapshot fingerprints;
- the exact coverage projection;
- policy fingerprint;
- canonical UTC evaluation time.

Full balances, positions, exposures, and quote rows are not projected. The four
snapshots remain independently timed parallel evidence and make no atomicity
claim.

## Decision Projection

`RiskDecision` is evaluator-owned and factory-only. Its bounded projection
contains:

- schema, outcome, and decision fingerprint;
- context, Order Intent, source signal, mapping, canonical descriptor, and
  policy fingerprints;
- canonical instrument ID;
- cash, position, and open-order account fingerprints;
- the common account fingerprint, or null when those accounts differ;
- all four snapshot fingerprints;
- canonical evaluation time;
- at most 32 ordered bounded findings.

It retains no full context or snapshot rows and confers no execution authority.
It privately retains the guarded canonical stage result for context-free
correspondence checks. The result contains an independent built-in tuple snapshot
of its constructor-time context fingerprint, outcome, findings, and common-account
state. Current semantics must match that tuple, and the result is checked against
a fresh computation whenever the canonical context is supplied. The tuple is
private, unprojected, unfingerprinted, unversioned, and is not cryptographic
protection. Guarded construction binds the original tuple by object identity in
a separate private immutable record, so a new equal or forged replacement is
rejected. The binding is also unprojected, unfingerprinted, and unversioned.

## Evaluation and Temporal Semantics

Input reconstruction occurs first and raises without producing a decision.
Intent validity is half-open: exact validity is active, one microsecond before
expiry is active, and exact expiry is rejected. Intent rejection stops all later
stages.

Resolution time may be before or equal to evaluation but not after it. Mapping
applicability is also half-open at evaluation. Resolution, mapping, or exact
trading-identity mismatch is indeterminate and stops later stages. Complete
account mismatch across cash, positions, and open orders is indeterminate and
stops freshness, coverage, and quote evaluation.

Freshness uses the released v0.58 evaluator for all four snapshots. Skew uses
only their four `as_of` values. Coverage collects every inadequate scope. Quote
sufficiency runs only with target-instrument coverage and never performs
direction-sensitive price selection.

13. `quote_future_dated`

18. `open_order_coverage_inadequate`
19. `quote_coverage_inadequate`
20. `quote_missing`

Findings sort by this order and then subject. A duplicate
`(reason_code, subject)` is invalid. Evidence fingerprints are unique, sorted,
and limited to four per finding. The decision maximum is exactly 32 findings.

## Outcomes and Correspondence

`approved` requires no findings and matching accounts. `rejected` requires
exactly one intent temporal finding. `indeterminate` requires nonempty
non-intent findings. Correspondence validation rejects stage mixtures, invalid
subjects or evidence, account/common-account disagreement, duplicate or
unordered findings, schemas, timestamps, context references, and stale
fingerprints.

Separate account fingerprints are always retained. The common value is present
only when all three match. When an earlier intent or resolution stage stops,
differing account fingerprints remain auditable and the common value remains
null without adding a downstream account finding.

## Compatibility and Boundaries

Released v0.55-v0.58 constructors, projections, schemas, fingerprints, and
application behavior remain unchanged. Historical Replay schema `1.1.0` and the
100/300/500-bar fingerprints remain compatibility gates.

This release adds no Decimal import or quantity arithmetic to the risk package,
financial risk, target delta, order netting, notional, cash sufficiency, buying
power, FX, margin, leverage, concentration, short authorization, `valid_until`,
revalidation helper, bundle, application operation, persistence, provider or
broker adapter, TradingView, human approval, execution planning, submission,
CLI, or Agent behavior.

A short target may be structurally approved but is not authorized. A later
answer requires new evidence, a new explicit evaluation time, a new context,
and a new evaluator run.
