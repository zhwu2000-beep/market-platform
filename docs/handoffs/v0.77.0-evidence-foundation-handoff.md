## Evidence semantic boundary

Evidence is a provenance-bearing representation of a source observation,
measurement, or assertion. The exact `EvidenceInformationClass` values are:

- `SOURCE_OBSERVATION`
- `SOURCE_MEASUREMENT`
- `SOURCE_ASSERTION`

Evidence is not truth, Personal Context, memory, portfolio state, derived
intelligence, an Authoritative Domain Result, a recommendation, Decision
History, task or workflow context, synthesis, or a trading action. A downstream
value does not become Source Evidence merely because it can be represented as
an assertion or assigned an Evidence information-class enum value.

## Origin authority

The exact `EvidenceAuthority` values are:

- `PLATFORM_ORIGIN`
- `EXTERNAL_ORIGIN`

Authority records origin only. Validation, admission, freshness, producer
metadata, transformations, predecessor lineage, and supersession cannot
promote or reconstruct it. Source authority must remain the exact governed
authority carried through the artifact correspondence.

## Governance trust root

`EvidenceContractDefinition` is descriptive only.
`EvidenceContractAuthorization` is descriptive only. Possessing or constructing
either record does not grant approval.

Production artifact minting requires exact positive membership in the private,
production-owned governed approval catalog. The production approval catalog in
v0.77.0 is empty. The release therefore provides the Evidence semantic and
lifecycle foundation while deliberately permitting no real production source
adapter to mint Evidence.

The released trust root has:

- no generic authorization issuer;
- no production catalog mutator;
- no alternate catalog injection;
- no public artifact-minting operation;
- only test-scoped governance fixtures, which are not production approval; and
- no path by which arbitrary semantic aliases become Evidence merely by
  selecting `SOURCE_MEASUREMENT` or another information class.

Python or module-private construction barriers support the boundary but are not
the governance trust root. The trust decision is exact membership in the closed
governed catalog. Every future production source adapter requires separate
governance approval before its exact authorization can enter that catalog.

## Artifact and contract correspondence

The foundation preserves immutable correspondence among:

- `EvidenceMaterialSchemaReference`;
- `EvidenceContractDefinition`;
- `EvidenceContractAuthorization`;
- `EvidenceSourceReference`;
- `EvidenceProvenance`;
- `EvidenceTemporalIdentity`;
- `EvidenceArtifact` and `EvidenceArtifactReference`;
- subject references; and
- direct predecessor lineage.

An approved authorization binds the exact authorization identity and version,
contract identity and version, evidence type, information class, material
schema identity/version/fingerprint, source identity/version/fingerprint, and
origin authority. Artifact construction derives the governed contract,
evidence type, information class, material schema, source, and authority from
the exact approved authorization. It separately enforces immutable
correspondence for provenance, temporal identity, subject references, and
predecessor lineage. Mixed or reconstructed governed dimensions fail closed.

## Temporal model

Temporal dimensions remain distinct rather than collapsing into one timestamp:

- observation or reference interval;
- effective or valid interval;
- publication or release time;
- source revision or vintage;
- receipt time;
- artifact creation time; and
- task or evaluation as-of time.

Freshness is an explicit evaluation at a stated as-of time against actual
artifact temporal data and the applicable freshness rule. It is not a
permanent boolean property of Evidence.

## Validation model

The exact validation concerns are:

- `IDENTITY`
- `INTEGRITY`
- `PROVENANCE`
- `AUTHORITY`
- `SCHEMA`
- `TEMPORAL_COHERENCE`
- `FRESHNESS_CONTRACT`
- `SOURCE_QUALITY`

The exact validation dispositions are `PASSED`, `REJECTED`, and
`UNABLE_TO_VALIDATE`. A validation requirement binds the exact concern, scope,
ruleset ID, and ruleset version. `SOURCE_QUALITY` is optional unless the
governing contract requires it.

Validation establishes the outcome of a specific policy check. It does not
authorize a source, promote origin authority, admit an artifact, or prove
permanent freshness.

## Admission and final consumability

The admission floor is non-downgradable. It requires the seven foundation
concerns (`IDENTITY`, `INTEGRITY`, `PROVENANCE`, `AUTHORITY`, `SCHEMA`,
`TEMPORAL_COHERENCE`, and `FRESHNESS_CONTRACT`), admission-time freshness,
task-time freshness, `ACTIVE` validity, and exact scope. A governing contract
may additionally require `SOURCE_QUALITY`; it cannot remove the foundation
floor.

`EvidenceAdmissionState` is an immutable evaluated/read model. It has no
aggregate fingerprint and no `full_record_resolution` proof flag. `ADMITTED`
alone is not a final consumability claim. Final consumability is a structural
consequence of the actual resolved artifact, ruleset, validation records,
freshness records, admission record, validity history, references, and exact
scope retained by the evaluation.

The sole root-public final-consumability evaluator is
`evaluate_evidence_admission_as_of`. The weaker reference-only evaluator is not
root-public and cannot claim final consumability.

## Validity v3

Validity v3 uses append-only immutable events with direct predecessor lineage,
actor and capability attribution, historical as-of replay, and exact artifact
and scope correspondence.

Its terminal states are:

- `EXPIRED`
- `WITHDRAWN`
- `REVOKED`

Once the same artifact version reaches a terminal state, it cannot reactivate.
Backdating, a different actor or capability, or an alternate lineage cannot
bypass the terminal-state rule.

## Artifact supersession

Artifact Supersession is distinct from construction provenance and distinct
from validity and admission. A supersession relation is scoped, carries
explicit temporal and provenance metadata, and preserves information class and
origin authority.

Supersession does not automatically invalidate or admit an artifact. It does
not change freshness, consumability, information class, or authority. Consumers
must evaluate each of those dimensions through its own lifecycle contract.

## Dependency and scope boundary

Evidence production code depends only on Evidence-local modules and the
established internal fingerprint utility. The v0.77.0 foundation deliberately
contains no:

- provider implementation, source adapter, or provider registry;
- persistence or database integration;
- transport, API, or MCP integration;
- Research migration or Agent runtime;
- Personal Intelligence implementation;
- Options, Macro, Fundamental, or Portfolio Evidence schema;
- trading or execution behavior; or
- generic graph engine.

These absences are architecture boundaries, not incomplete implicit features.

## Enduring hardening lessons

Future work must preserve these conclusions:

1. Internal consistency is not semantic authorization.
2. Python/private visibility is not itself a governance trust root.
3. Evidence semantics require positive governed authorization, not a denylist
   of forbidden names.
4. Source authority must be governed, not reconstructed from caller-selected
   enum values.
5. `is_consumable` must be a structural consequence of exact resolved
   constituents, never a caller-supplied proof flag.
6. Validation requirements must bind exact policy identity and version.
7. Freshness must correspond to actual artifact temporal data.
8. Terminal validity cannot be bypassed by backdating or alternate lineage.
9. Supersession does not imply invalidity or authority promotion.
10. Personal or derived intelligence must never be laundered into Evidence.

## Future governed source-adapter work

The next natural architecture step is separately governed source-adapter work.
For each actual source, that work must define:

- the exact Evidence contract;
- the material schema;
- source identity;
- origin authority;
- adapter trust and governance approval;
- validation policy;
- freshness contract; and
- lifecycle integration.

Any adapter that needs production Evidence minting must be separately reviewed
before its exact authorization is admitted to the production governed approval
catalog. This release and handoff do not implement or pre-approve those
adapters.

Future domain results remain downstream of Evidence. Authoritative Domain
Results, derived intelligence, recommendations, and other downstream products
must not be reclassified as Source Evidence.
