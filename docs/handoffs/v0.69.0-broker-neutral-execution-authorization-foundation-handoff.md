## Authorization Semantics

Authorization is success-only. It replays the existing deterministic structural
risk evaluator exactly once to validate the supplied evidence; no second risk
engine or authorization-specific risk calculation exists. APPROVED with exact
empty findings is required. REJECTED or INDETERMINATE combined with downstream
execution artifacts is contradictory public input and raises
`ExecutionPlanningValidationError`, not
`ExecutionPlanningUnavailableError`. v0.69 contains no use of
`ExecutionPlanningUnavailableError`.

`BrokerNeutralExecutionAuthorizationPolicyIdentity` is immutable platform
rule-set identity/provenance. It has no runtime protocol, behavioral callback,
configuration, or `configuration_fingerprint`. Authorization retained-state
validation reconstructs the policy identity from ID/version and verifies its
fingerprint, so contradictory policy metadata cannot survive top-level
refingerprinting. A coherent rewrite to another completely valid identity
remains within the documented value-semantic boundary.

The common `evidence_as_of` is derived from exact upstream evidence-time
correspondence. Caller-supplied `authorization_as_of` is canonical UTC and
must satisfy `authorization_as_of >= evidence_as_of`. There is no system-clock
read, freshness threshold, expiration, or validity interval.

## Stage-Trust Boundary

Direct inputs are fully revalidated: risk context, risk decision, position
translation, order specification, structural compatibility result, native
mapping, and authorization policy identity. Risk replay, account identity,
common evidence time, specification/compatibility/mapping correspondence,

Capability profile and InstrumentMapping semantics remain transitively bound by
the validated compatibility result and native mapping rather than becoming
direct inputs. Structural risk policy and account evidence remain nested in the
risk context; the execution instruction remains nested in the specification.
Opaque native tokens remain mapper-conformance data and are never interpreted.

The result retains no upstream source object, mapper, registry, attestation, or
weak reference. It proves current canonical value consistency, not original
factory, evaluator-code, or mapper-code authenticity.
