## Identity Contracts

`StateSnapshotSourceIdentity` uses visible ASCII without whitespace:

- `source_id`: 1-128 characters;
- `source_version`: 1-64 characters;
- `configuration_fingerprint`: null or `sha256:` plus 64 lowercase hex digits.

Its fingerprint covers schema, ID, version, and optional configuration. It does
not prove authentication or trust and has no source-role field.

`TradingAccountIdentity` uses:

- institution namespace: `[a-z][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}`;
- opaque account ID: visible ASCII without whitespace, 1-128 characters;
- environment: exactly `paper` or `live`;
- base currency: `[A-Z]{3}`.

Its fingerprint and projection include every field, including the full account
ID. Future transport layers own authorization and redaction.

## Decimal Contract

Constructors accept exact `Decimal` instances only. Values are finite,
fixed-point, unrounded, context-independent, and never converted through float.
Projected sizes are computed from `Decimal.as_tuple()` before formatting:

- fixed-point text: at most 256 characters;
- total digit characters: at most 128;
- fractional digit characters: at most 64.

The minus sign counts toward text length but not digit count. Positive zero,
including huge-exponent zero, becomes exact `Decimal("0")` and text `"0"`.
Negative zero is always rejected. Cash is signed and permits zero. Position and
open-order quantities are signed and nonzero. Prices are strictly positive.
All Decimal projections are canonical fixed-point strings.

```text
 "remaining_quantity": "-2"}

 "bid": null | "...", "ask": null | "...", "last": null | "..."}
```

Every retained `CanonicalInstrumentId` and nested record is independently
reconstructed through its released/public constructor. Snapshots require exact
field, Decimal tuple, and complete projection correspondence. Numeric equality
alone is insufficient.

## Snapshot Projections and Fingerprints

Account cash, position, and open-order snapshots project:

```text
{
  "schema_version": ".../v1",
  "account": {...complete identity and fingerprint...},
  "source": {...complete identity and fingerprint...},
  "as_of": "...+00:00",
  "balances" | "positions" | "exposures": [...],
  "fingerprint": "sha256:..."
}
```

Market quote snapshots omit account and use `quotes`. Every fingerprint covers
the schema, complete source identity, canonical `as_of`, and ordered rows;
account-owned snapshots additionally cover the complete account identity.
Caller-supplied fingerprints are impossible.

## Collections

All collection inputs accept only exact built-in `list` or `tuple`. Subclasses,
custom sequences or iterables, generators, sets, mappings, strings, and bytes
are rejected before iteration. Counts are checked before copying or sorting.

| Snapshot | Maximum | Sort key | Duplicate key |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Account cash | 32 | currency | currency |
| Positions | 10,000 | canonical instrument ID | canonical instrument ID |
| Open-order exposure | 10,000 | order ID, instrument ID | order ID |
| Market quotes | 10,000 | canonical instrument ID | canonical instrument ID |

Duplicates are rejected without aggregation or overwrite. All collections may
be empty. Empty is exact source-reported state, not proof of risk sufficiency.

## Time and Quote Semantics

Every snapshot owns one caller-supplied aware `as_of`, physically stored as
`datetime.UTC` with microseconds preserved and projected using `+00:00`. There
is no current-time, local-time, capture, receipt, or generation timestamp.

A quote requires at least one of bid, ask, or last. Supplied prices are strictly
positive. Bid cannot exceed ask; last need not be inside the spread. Quote
currency remains part of the separately retained canonical instrument evidence.

## Pure Temporal Evaluation

`evaluate_snapshot_freshness(snapshot_as_of, evaluation_as_of, maximum_age)`:

- snapshot after evaluation: `future_dated`;
- age less than or equal to maximum: `fresh`;
- age greater than maximum: `stale`.

Zero maximum age is valid; negative age limits are rejected.

`evaluate_snapshot_skew(snapshot_times, maximum_skew)` accepts an exact list or
tuple of 1-32 aware timestamps:

- `max(time) - min(time) <= maximum_skew`: `coherent`;
- otherwise: `excessive_skew`.

A singleton is coherent. Exact maximum skew is coherent. Future-datedness is
not a skew classification. Both functions are clock-free and unversioned.

## Error Hierarchy

- `TradingStateDomainError` is the domain base.
- `TradingStateValidationError` covers malformed input and resource bounds.
- `TradingStateDuplicateError` is its collection-duplicate subtype.
- `TradingStateCorrespondenceError` covers impossible retained state.

Unexpected programming exceptions are not broadly translated.

## Compatibility and Boundaries

No v0.55 trading, v0.56 application, or v0.57 instrument source is modified.
Their schemas, projections, fingerprints, and behavior remain authoritative.
Analytical state/signals, observations, Research, Replay, Artifact, Experiment,
providers, benchmark schema, and package version are unchanged.

The four snapshots have independent sources and times and make no atomicity,
freshness, broker-authority, or risk-sufficiency claim. V0.58 adds no bundle,
application operation, persistence, adapter, broker/provider access,
TradingView/HTTP behavior, authentication, risk rule or decision, execution,
CLI, Agent, filesystem access, network access, UUID, randomness, or clock.

Future risk evidence must retain the exact Order Intent, mapping, canonical
instrument, account, required snapshot, risk-policy, and decision-time
identities. Changed fingerprints invalidate silent reuse.
