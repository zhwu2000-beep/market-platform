## Domain Contract

`LimitPriceChoice(limit_price, trading_currency)` is frozen, slotted,
caller-authored, timeless, broker-neutral, non-executable, and independent of
style, instruction, instrument, account, and quote evidence. Its public fields
are exactly `limit_price`, `trading_currency`, derived `schema_version`,
and derived `fingerprint`. Its schema is `limit_price_choice/v1`.

Price requires an exact built-in finite strictly positive `Decimal` under the
released v0.58 bounds: 128 digit characters, 64 fractional digits, and 256
fixed-point characters. Tuple preflight rejects hostile exponent expansion
before formatting. Projection removes insignificant fractional zeros without
rounding and is independent of ambient Decimal context.

Currency requires an exact built-in uppercase ASCII value matching
`[A-Z]{3}`. It records denomination only and does not prove live registry,
instrument, settlement, FX, or broker correspondence.

## Correspondence and Threat Boundary

Projection validates exact canonical retained price and currency, exact schema
and fingerprint, and every required slot. Malformed or deleted retained state
raises `ExecutionPlanningCorrespondenceError`; unrelated programming
exceptions propagate.

This caller-authored value has no token, constructor-state tuple, identity
binding, retained source, factory, provenance, or cryptographic-authenticity
claim. A caller may legitimately construct any valid price/currency pair.

## Scope

MARKET consumes no price artifact. Future LIMIT specification construction must
require one choice and separately validate style compatibility,
instrument-currency correspondence, tick size, and broker capability. V0.63
adds no quote derivation, rounding, TIF, session, position effect, generic
constraint container, order specification, authorization, broker request,
submission, persistence, or lifecycle semantics.
