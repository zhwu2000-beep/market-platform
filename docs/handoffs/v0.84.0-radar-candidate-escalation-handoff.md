## 3. Architectural purpose and accepted ADRs

The conceptual progression is:

```text
Radar observation
→ meaningful change
→ passive Candidate
→ recoverable Candidate delivery
→ STOP at durable Pending work
```

This is a capability foundation, not an automatically wired production flow.
Meaningful change alone does not establish Candidate eligibility, and Pending
does not mean delivered or research started.

| Accepted ADR | Implemented boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [ADR0050: Radar Candidate and Escalation Boundary](../adr/0050_radar_candidate_and_escalation_boundary.md) | Passive Candidate, fixed eligibility policy, pure evaluation and separation from governed authority. |
| [ADR0051: Radar Candidate Delivery and Recovery Boundary](../adr/0051_radar_candidate_delivery_and_recovery_boundary.md) | Owned source publication, durable decision/Pending records, restart reconciliation and overwrite interlock. |

## 4. Frozen Candidate boundary — ADR0050

`application/radar_candidate.py` owns the passive Candidate and pure synchronous
`evaluate_radar_candidate` function. Candidate means eligible for governed
research consideration. It is not a trade instruction, recommendation, research
conclusion or Evidence admission, and grants no research, strategy, broker or
execution authority. Candidate formation must not bypass existing governance.

- `radar_candidate/v1` retains a detached, revalidated source observation-state
  snapshot, applied eligibility policy and deterministic fingerprint. Fingerprints
  establish correspondence, not execution, acceptance or bearer authority.
- The closed policy is `selected_meaningful_transition / "1" /
  radar_candidate_eligibility_policy/v1`; no configurable ranking or directional
  preference is introduced.
- Eligibility requires actual `SELECTED`, application `SAVED`, a corresponding
  committed meaningful `STATE_TRANSITION`, approved observation/policy semantics,
  and an actually executed qualifying Session/Content Trigger PASS. Full
  instrument, Profile, execution, checkpoint and state correspondence is checked.
- All six changes among EMA8/EMA20 `ABOVE`, `EQUAL` and `BELOW` qualify under the
  accepted transition semantics. FILTERED transitions, baselines, unchanged
  results and failures do not produce Candidates; no historical backfill or
  resurrection of a prior filtered event is introduced.
- For valid inputs, reason precedence is `NOT_SELECTED`, `NOT_ADVANCED`,
  `NO_MEANINGFUL_TRANSITION`, then `ELIGIBLE` with Candidate. Malformed or
  contradictory input raises rather than becoming a normal negative decision.
- Evaluation performs no I/O, acquisition, persistence or governed research.
  Caller-constructed values are not portable proof of trusted execution or commit.

Production activation remains deferred.

## 5. Frozen delivery and recovery boundary — ADR0051

`application/radar_candidate_delivery.py` supplies the owned recovery composition;
`application/radar_candidate_delivery_store.py` supplies narrow local immutable
record persistence. The optional coordinator in `radar/application.py` runs
recovery before a new evaluation and delegates validated publication before the
checkpoint write. Without it, the existing application path remains unchanged.
Radar observation code does not decide Candidate eligibility.

The implemented ordering is:

```text
PreparedSourceIntent
→ checkpoint publication
→ AcceptedSource
→ CandidateDecisionRecord
→ PendingCandidateWork, only if eligible
→ permit the next evaluation / checkpoint overwrite
```

Prepared retains exact execution, proposed state, predecessor witness and the
policy selected before publication. Prepared alone is not accepted observation
or Candidate authority. Acceptance is established through owned publication or
exact reconciliation; only then is the trusted source reconstructed for Candidate
evaluation. Negative decisions are persisted too. Pending retains the complete
Candidate through its decision and binds its source recovery identity; Candidate
fingerprint is the Pending key. Source identity and Candidate identity remain
distinct.

For unresolved work, an exact current proposal establishes publication without
republishing; an exact expected predecessor permits publication of the retained
proposal. Unexpected, unreadable or corrupt authority fails closed. Recovery
uses the original retained source and policy without reacquiring data, rerunning
Gates, resampling source times or substituting today's policy. Pre-activation
checkpoints without owned Prepared context are not backfill sources.

Unresolved source work blocks superseding evaluation/publication for the
coordinated instrument until acceptance, decision and eligible Pending work are
durable. Pending need not be consumed before a later observation. Later checkpoint
replacement cannot erase the retained source, decision or Pending records.

The store requires an absolute, caller-provisioned trusted root; it creates no
root. Strict schemas, canonical correspondence, duplicate-key rejection,
filename/content checks and retained-record link checks fail closed. Delivery
root/record symlinks, reparse points and non-regular entries are rejected;
checkpoint path-safety failures block recovery. Missing/unreadable authority is
not silently treated as an empty queue. Unexpected multiple unresolved intents
also fail closed.

Publication uses same-filesystem temporary files, flush/file-fsync and no-clobber
hard links for immutable records. Same key and identical full payload succeeds
idempotently; different payload conflicts. Unpublished temporary files carry no
authority. A failed acceptance write after checkpoint publication is unresolved
completion, not observation rollback.

The guarantee is recoverable process-interruption safety with retained trusted
local filesystem contents, one non-overlapping owner per instrument and no bypass
writer. It does not claim directory-fsync, universal power/device-loss durability,
concurrent-writer coordination, malicious-tamper resistance or exactly-once
delivery/research. The foundation stops at durable Pending: no consumer,
acknowledgment, worker claims, leases, expiry, deletion or compaction is implemented.

## 6. Production non-activation

Current production remains **NON-ACTIVATING**:

- No Candidate production from the single-symbol path.
- No Candidate production from the batch path.
- No automatic Pending writes.
- No automatic recovery root creation.
- No Candidate/delivery package-root export.

- No broker/execution authority.

coordinator; `run_radar_batch` uses that unwired single-symbol path. Production
responses remain observation/application results. The optional seam and local
record capability do not activate Candidate-to-research orchestration.
