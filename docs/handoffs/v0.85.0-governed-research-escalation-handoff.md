## 3. Architectural purpose and frozen ADR0052 boundary

v0.85 provides the non-activating Governed Research Escalation Readiness foundation
after v0.84 Candidate / recoverable Pending delivery. The conceptual chain is:

```text
Radar observation
→ meaningful change
→ passive Candidate
→ recoverable Pending
→ trusted Pending resolution
→ governed material / current task-context resolution
→ exact Candidate ↔ governed-material correspondence
→ bounded READY / BLOCKED / REFUSED assessment
→ STOP
```

[ADR0052](../adr/0052_governed_research_escalation_readiness_boundary.md) freezes
readiness as distinct from Candidate eligibility, Evidence Admission and research
activation. `READY` describes prerequisites that held for the recorded context;
it is non-authoritative and permits no research execution. A future invoker must
independently revalidate current Pending trust, material, governance, task
freshness, policy and timing. No bearer authorization token exists.

The boundary authenticates existing owned records and delegates governance to
existing owners. Candidate content identity establishes correspondence, not
Evidence authenticity or acquisition provenance. Normalized historical-content
identity and Bridge representation fingerprint retain their separate meanings.
Matching content from independent acquisitions does not establish a shared HTTP
response or permit provenance substitution.

Readiness preserves original observation, query/receipt, effective, knowledge,
execution and availability times. It neither repairs missing authority nor
creates a durable readiness queue/store, execution journal or consumer lifecycle.
Result explanation depends on retained results and trusted owner histories;
cross-restart readiness-result recovery and complete audit retention are not
guaranteed. ADR0052's proposal/future-implementation wording records its authoring
checkpoint; this handoff records the completed implementation without changing
the ADR or granting activation.

### Slice 1 — Frozen readiness contract

The exact closed policy identity is:

- `policy_id`: `exact_governed_250_session_technical_readiness`.
- `behavioral_revision`: `"1"`.
- Schema: `radar_research_escalation_readiness_policy/v1`.

Requests contain untrusted lookup/context references. Immutable assessments keep
supplied references distinct from actually evaluated references, structured
findings, policy identity and actual execution times. Outcomes are `READY`,
`BLOCKED` and `REFUSED`. Explicit `NOT_CHECKED` semantics prevent an early failure

### Slice 2 — Trusted Pending resolution

Trusted owner lookup authenticates the complete retained graph:

```text
Prepared → Accepted → eligible Decision → Pending
```

Resolution validates source recovery identity, accepted publication, eligibility
policy, Candidate and retained links. Detached values or matching fingerprints
cannot replace owner lookup. Invalid lookup identities and graph contradictions
are refused; known owner/Pending unavailability blocks. Failure precedence keeps
contradictions from being disguised as ordinary missing work, and Pending failure
stops later readiness checks.

Resolution is read-only and non-consuming: it does not publish unfinished work,
write/acknowledge Pending or create a recovery root. Restart resolution requires
retained authentic owners and records. Historical Pending remains resolvable
after later checkpoint replacement; the latest checkpoint does not replace its
retained source graph.

### Slice 3 — Governed-context resolution

Resolution uses retained governed material and authentic owner histories, current
task governance, Qualification and exact task freshness. It requires the same
canonical instrument with mapping coverage, exactly 250 ordered completed
sessions including the triggering session, exact normalized historical-content
identity, authentic material/construction lineage and retained Bridge
correspondence. Row count or matching endpoints alone is insufficient.

Current consumability and task freshness are checked for the intended use,
analysis context and technical profile. Historical Qualification/Bridge records
do not replace current governance checks. Bridge authenticates transformation
and complete no-drop row correspondence and remains **non-Technical**; resolving
a Technical request reference does not invoke Technical or its analyzer.

No acquisition/refetch, material substitution, missing-history synthesis or
Candidate-to-data shortcut occurs. Unsupported same-day/post-close timing fails
closed. The B1 contradiction-precedence fix ensures demonstrable task/profile,
material-lineage or retained Bridge contradictions take precedence over missing
or stale prerequisites. Missing authority alone does not manufacture REFUSED.

## 7. Same-response post-close limitation

The existing Evidence ingress contract cannot automatically legitimize the same
post-close source response including the triggering session under unsupported
timing. Ingress excludes the New York query civil date and requires query time
no later than response receipt. Unsupported timing is refused; missing admissible
material blocks readiness. Passage of time cannot retroactively repair provenance.

Prohibited workarounds remain:

- Backdating.
- Timestamp rewriting.
- Dropping the triggering row.
- A 249-row fallback.
- Later refetch represented as original provenance.
- Elapsed-time retroactive legitimacy.

A separate future acquisition-contract decision is required if this capability
is ever added. v0.85 does not redesign ingress or require every authentic Pending
to become READY.
