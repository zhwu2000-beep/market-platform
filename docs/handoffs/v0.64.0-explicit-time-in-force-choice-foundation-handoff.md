## Domain Contract

`TimeInForceChoice(time_in_force)` is frozen, slotted, bounded,
caller-authored, timeless, broker-neutral, and non-executable. Its public fields
are exactly `time_in_force`, derived `schema_version`, and derived
`fingerprint`. Its schema is `time_in_force_choice/v1`.

DAY requests eligibility through the applicable downstream-resolved order day.
GTC requests cross-day/session persistence until canceled, subject to later
rules. IOC requests immediate matching to the available extent and cancellation
of any remainder. FOK requests immediate full execution or cancellation without
partial fill. All four are requests rather than broker-support, acceptance,
persistence, liquidity, fill, or cancellation-timing guarantees.

Missing input never means DAY. Exact enum input is required; strings and foreign
enums are not converted.

## Correspondence and Threat Boundary

Projection validates the exact retained enum and every required slot. Retained
schema and fingerprint require exact built-in strings before equality checks;
equality-spoofing objects and string subclasses are rejected. The projection is
JSON-safe and contains only schema, lowercase TIF, and fingerprint.

This caller-authored value has no token, constructor-state tuple, identity
binding, retained source, factory, provenance, or cryptographic-authenticity
claim. A caller may independently construct any valid choice.

## GTD, Session, Compatibility, and Authority

GTD is unavailable. A future milestone must define explicit expiry source,
canonical timestamp representation, comparison anchor, and broker/calendar
constraints. Signal/intent expiry, instruction plan time, and wall-clock time
are not repurposed.

The choice has no session, extended-hours, style, limit-price, instruction,
instrument, account, timestamp, or capability coupling. Future specification
construction owns required style/price/TIF/session correspondence; later
capability validation owns broker and venue support.

The choice is not an order specification, authorization, submission authority,
broker request, persistence result, cancellation command, or lifecycle state.
