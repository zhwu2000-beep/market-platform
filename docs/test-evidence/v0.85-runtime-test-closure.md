## Path D: matching content with independent provenance

X is the Radar acquisition associated with the authentic Candidate/Pending; Y is
a later independent acquisition used to establish governed material. Y is not
represented as X's HTTP response or original evidence. Exact normalized content
correspondence is necessary but does not authenticate Y by itself: Construction,
Validation, admission Freshness, Admission, ACTIVE Validity, exact task Freshness,
Qualification and Bridge retain their existing owned meanings.

The frozen contract requires the same canonical instrument, mapping coverage and
exact ordered 250 completed sessions including the trigger. Matching row counts
or endpoints alone do not suffice. The Bridge representation fingerprint and
normalized historical-content identity remain separate identities.

Path D does not repair the unsupported same-response post-close path. No
backdating, timestamp rewriting, 249-row fallback, trigger-row removal, hidden
refetch represented as original evidence, or retroactive legitimacy is permitted.
Acquisition and governance availability must honestly precede the assessment's
applicable context. See ADR0052 Decisions 2–6 and 11.
